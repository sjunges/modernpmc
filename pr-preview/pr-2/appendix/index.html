6.1Latticesdiscuss!¶

Let (L,≤)(L, \leq)(L,≤) be a partially ordered set, and let X⊆LX \subseteq LX⊆L.

  • An element u∈Lu \in Lu∈L is an upper bound of XXX if x≤ux \leq ux≤u for all x∈Xx \in Xx∈X.

  • An element l∈Ll \in Ll∈L is a lower bound of XXX if l≤xl \leq xl≤x for all x∈Xx \in Xx∈X.

  • The least upper bound (or join) of XXX, denoted ⋁X\bigvee X⋁X, is an upper bound uuu of XXX such that for any other upper bound u′u'u′ of XXX, u≤u′u \leq u'u≤u′.

  • The greatest lower bound (or meet) of XXX, denoted ⋀X\bigwedge X⋀X, is a lower bound lll of XXX such that for any other lower bound l′l'l′ of XXX, l′≤ll' \leq ll′≤l.

Definition 3 ((complete) latttices)

A lattice (L,≤)(L, \leq)(L,≤) is a partially ordered set in where each pair x,y∈Lx, y \in Lx,y∈L has a join and a meet in LLL.
A lattice is complete, if every subset X⊆LX \subseteq LX⊆L has a join ⋁X\bigvee X⋁X and a meet ⋀X\bigwedge X⋀X in LLL.

6.2Monotone and Continuous Operatorsdiscuss!¶

Let (L,≤)(L, \leq)(L,≤) be a partially ordered set. Operators are functions from LLL to LLL.

Definition 4 (monotone operator)

An operator Ψ ⁣:L→L\Psi\colon L \to LΨ:L→L is monotone if for all x,y∈Lx, y \in Lx,y∈L:

x≤y  ⟹  Ψ(x)≤Ψ(y)x \leq y \implies \Psi(x) \leq \Psi(y)x≤y⟹Ψ(x)≤Ψ(y)
Definition 5 (ω\omegaω-continuous operator)

Let (L,≤)(L, \leq)(L,≤) be a complete lattice and Ψ ⁣:L→L\Psi\colon L \to LΨ:L→L a monotone operator.
The operator FFF is ω\omegaω-continuous if it preserves suprema of countable ascending chains. Formally: Let (xi)i≥0(x_i)_{i \ge 0}(xi​)i≥0​ be a countable ascending chain in LLL, i.e.,

x0≤x1≤x2≤…x_0 \leq x_1 \leq x_2 \leq \dotsx0​≤x1​≤x2​≤…

Ψ(⋁i≥0xi)=⋁i≥0Ψ(xi)\Psi\Big(\bigvee_{i \ge 0} x_i\Big) = \bigvee_{i \ge 0} \Psi(x_i)Ψ(i≥0⋁​xi​)=i≥0⋁​Ψ(xi​)

The intuition here is that applying FFF to the “limit” of an ascending chain is the same as taking the limit of applying FFF to each element. This property is crucial for infinite lattices, which we encounter in analysing MDPs.
